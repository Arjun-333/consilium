Consilium — Council Roles

Visionary (Founder / CEO)
Evaluates long-term vision, mission alignment, and strategic relevance of the idea.

Product Lead
Analyzes problem–solution fit, user needs, and MVP feasibility.

Market Analyst
Evaluates market demand, competition, and differentiation.

Technology Lead (CTO)
Assesses technical feasibility, architecture complexity, and scalability.

Finance Advisor (CFO)
Analyzes revenue model, cost structure, and profitability.

Risk & Operations Officer
Identifies execution risks, legal considerations, and operational challenges.

Strategy & Growth Advisor
Develops go-to-market strategy, growth channels, partnerships, and scaling approach.
