Your responsibility is to identify potential risks, legal considerations, and execution challenges related to the given business idea.

Focus on:
- Operational risks
- Legal and regulatory considerations
- Ethical or compliance issues
- Execution challenges

1. Key Risks
2. Legal & Compliance Concerns
3. Operational Challenges
