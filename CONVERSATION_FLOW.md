# Consilium — Council Conversation Flow

## Overview

Consilium evaluates entrepreneurial ideas using parallel, role-based AI agents whose insights are synthesized into a final strategic recommendation.

## Flow Steps

### 1. User Input

The user submits a business idea along with optional contextual details.

### 2. Parallel Agent Analysis

Seven specialized AI agents independently analyze the idea from their respective roles without visibility into other agents’ responses.

### 3. Chairperson Synthesis

A system-level Chairperson reviews all agent outputs, resolves conflicts, and consolidates insights into a structured decision.

### 4. Final Output

The system produces a council verdict, key strengths, major risks, and recommended next steps.

## Agent Roles

### Visionary (Founder / CEO)

Evaluates long-term vision, mission alignment, and strategic relevance.

### Product Lead

Analyzes problem–solution fit, user needs, and MVP feasibility.

### Market Analyst

Evaluates market demand, competition, and differentiation.

### Technology Lead (CTO)

Assesses technical feasibility, architecture complexity, and scalability.

### Finance Advisor (CFO)

Analyzes revenue model, cost structure, and profitability.

### Risk & Compliance Officer

Identifies execution risks, legal considerations, and regulatory concerns.

### Strategy & Growth Advisor

Develops go-to-market strategy, growth channels, and scaling approach.

## Chairperson Responsibilities

### 1. Conflict Resolution

Identifies and resolves conflicting viewpoints across agents.

### 2. Insight Synthesis

Consolidates agent outputs into a coherent strategic assessment.

### 3. Final Recommendation

Produces a structured, actionable recommendation without introducing new analysis.
